Your customers reach out on their terms by phone, by text, during business hours and after. The question is whether your business is there when they do. A call comes in after hours. A text goes unanswered. A customer gets put on hold and hangs up. Today, RingCentral is expanding AI Receptionist (AIR), an AI agent that answers calls and texts 24/7, to help businesses handle more of these moments across new channels and workflows, including e-commerce, scheduling, messaging, and overflow handling.
