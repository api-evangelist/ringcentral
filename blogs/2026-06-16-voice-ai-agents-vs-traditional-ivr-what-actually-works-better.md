This article examines how voice AI agents outperform legacy interactive voice response (IVR) systems, noting that voice is involved in 82% of all customer interactions. It explains that AI-powered solutions can understand natural speech and resolve requests end-to-end, whereas traditional IVR only routes calls through fixed menus, and includes case studies showing measurable gains in productivity and customer satisfaction.
