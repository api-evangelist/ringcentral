A practical comparison of unified communications platforms that replace, not just supplement, Microsoft Teams. Searching for a Teams alternative usually starts with a specific operational gap, not a pricing spreadsheet. For most enterprise organizations, the trigger is realizing that Teams was never built for external communications: business phone capabilities are limited, SMS/MMS support is weak, and extending Teams to customer-facing interactions requires bolting on additional vendors. The second trigger is risk.
