How to design self-service channels that resolve issues, reduce agent volume, and keep customers satisfied. A large share of your agents’ time goes to answering the same questions—queries on account status, order updates, password resets, appointment scheduling. Given an easy path, most customers would prefer to handle these tasks themselves.
