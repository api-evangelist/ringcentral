Client communication rarely happens within standard business hours for professional services firms. Law firms often hear from prospective clients who reach out after hours for immediate legal guidance. Consultants receive time-sensitive messages while in client meetings or traveling. Cybersecurity and IT firms must stay in constant contact during active incidents or system disruptions, when rapid response is critical.
